Bend mode, persistent OSD, record UI. No custom boot screen - this ROM has no startup image to replace.

Card volume label RWD_A410 - it must NOT be CHDK. On FAT the volume label
is an entry in the root directory, so a card labelled CHDK collides with
the CHDK directory itself: modules stop loading and settings stop saving.
