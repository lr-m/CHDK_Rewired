CHDK Rewired - PowerShot A480
firmware: 100b

DISKBOOT.BIN md5 3afa42415f89e3b19b825bcc687c2b99

Bend mode, persistent OSD, record UI, custom sounds, custom boot screen with animation.

Card volume label RWD_A480 - it must NOT be CHDK. On FAT the volume label
is an entry in the root directory, so a card labelled CHDK collides with
the CHDK directory itself: modules stop loading and settings stop saving.
